Tight plume: long, hot, fast. Wide plume: short and broad, less thrust. Dumping cuts the engine; drift clear, then relight into the fuel cloud. Enemy lasers can set it off too. RCS is cold gas: weak, limited, and safe to use inside a fuel cloud. Green scrap repairs your hull, but your exhaust blows it away. The handbrake is an all-stop: the nose and side RCS thrusters together push straight against your drift, sharing one thruster's worth of output. While held it takes over the RCS, so strafe and retro do nothing. Drifting tail-first, it can only kill the sideways part.
